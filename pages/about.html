Meet the astronaut

Hello! I'm Natana, a designer based in Porto, Portugal.

My journey spans different orbits of design, leading me to explore a multidisciplinary universe that connects UX/UI & Product Design, Graphic & Fashion Design, Illustration, Animation, Mobile and Front-end Development.

Throughout this creative journey, I transform ideas into engaging, intuitive, and visually striking digital experiences, always with the user as the starting point and guide of the mission. I create universes where strategy, aesthetics, and technology meet to bring to life playful, functional, and memorable experiences. Explore some of my projects and join me on this stellar journey through my design universe.
